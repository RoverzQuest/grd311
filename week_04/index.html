Butternut Squash Feta Soup

Ingredients

  • 3 cups butternut squash, peeled and cubed
  • 1 large sweet onion, cut into wedges
  • 1 sprig fresh sage
  • 1 sprig fresh rosemary
  • 1 sprig fresh thyme
  • Kosher salt
  • Freshly cracked black pepper to taste
  • 1 whole garlic head, cut in half lengthwise
  • 1 6-ounce feta block
  • 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
  • 4 cups vegetable broth, or chicken broth

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F
  2. Combine butternut squash, onion, sage, rosemary and thyme in a large baking dish & toss with 2½ tablespoons olive oil, ½ teaspoon salt & fresh cracked black pepper
  3. Nestle each half of the garlic head into the vegetables, cut side down
  4. Create a hole in the center and place feta block, drizzle with remaining olive oil
  5. Roast until squash is tender and feta is caramelized, about 30-35 minutes
  6. Remove herbs and squeeze out roasted garlic
  7. Transfer vegetables and cheese to the blender; add broth and blend until smooth
  8. Warm on the stove when ready to serve

Peanut Butter Cookies

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup butter unsalted
  • 1 cup peanut butter
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 large egg
  • 3/4 tsp baking powder

Instructions

  1. Preheat over to 350F
  2. Sift flour and baking powder together then whisk to combine
  3. Cream butter and sugars in a stand mixer fitted with a paddle attachment
  4. You can add an optional 1/4-1/2 tsp sea salt
  5. Add peanut butter and mix until incorporated
  6. Mix in egg and vanilla extract then add flour mixture and beat until incorporated
  7. Roll dough into one inch balls and place on baking sheet lined with parchment paper
  8. Flatten cookies with a fork in a criss-cross pattern
  9. Bake cookies for about 10 minutes
  10. Allow cookies to cool completely on baking sheet, they need to set up before being transferred

Pecan Pumpkin Loaf

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 5 1/2 teaspoons pumpkin spice
  • 2/3 cup sugar
  • 2/3 cup brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 3 large egg whites
  • 2/3 cups canned pumpkin puree
  • 1 cup pecans, roughly chopped

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350 degrees F
  2. Lightly spray a loaf pan with non-stick cooking spray and if you prefer, line the pan with parchment paper
  3. Set aside
  4. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, pumpkin spice, salt, baking soda, and baking powder
  5. In a large bowl, beat the sugar, brown sugar, milk, and egg whites until well blended
  6. Add the pumpkin puree and incorporate it into the mixture
  7. Add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and fold together using a spatula
  8. Finally, fold in the pecans
  9. Pour the batter into the loaf pan and tap the pan firmly on the countertop to settle the batter into the pan and to eliminate bubbles
  10. Bake for 55 minutes
  11. Allow the loaf to cool for 5 minutes in the loaf pan before removing to a wire cooling rack to completely cool
